# Surface: marketing homepage

Mode: Persuade
Audience: Nigerian secondary-school principals, NGO programme leads, training-hall owners
Job: believe Orangebook is the register for their hall, then apply for a custom quote
Action: Apply for a quote (#apply). Log in is secondary.
Proof: real Ikeja Secondary desks (dashboard, parent record, sample First Term report). Generated office plate is illustrative, not a named customer.
Constraints: custom quote only; no Free/Basic/Pro; no fake logos; no child-face stock; no glass island or word-reveal; Helvetica Neue across marketing and desks; 8px controls / 12px cards; login split ink-on-white must keep working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Orangebook is the hall’s mark book, shown as the working desk, not as a dark literary brochure. The page refuses the black couplet + HTML card + FAQ column of 2012 education SaaS.

OWN-WORLD: Light Apple field. White canvas, #f5f5f7 bands, ink #1d1d1f, mute #6e6e73, Helvetica Neue, 8px controls and 12px cards and shots. Product UI screenshots and one Lagos principal-office plate are the pictures. Report card stays a paper object on the results band. Thin sticky bar. Apply and login buttons are 8px, not pills.

STORY: Visitor sees one login, sees the desk, walks attendance then results then parents, then applies. They believe the book already exists. They act on Apply for a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin light bar (wordmark, Attendance, Results, Parents, Apply, Log in). Below: left-aligned display line “One login for the whole school.” Lede of one sentence. Black Apply, text Log in, note on custom fees. Under that a large shadowed screenshot of the Ikeja principal dashboard. Primary action is Apply in the bar and the hero.

FORM: Canon / standing exit. Craft bar is Apple, Microsoft, Salesforce, NVIDIA homepages. Seed key: none (user named Fortune-25 companies; concept-seed skipped). Signature interaction: none beyond sticky bar and FAQ open. Motion is color/opacity 150ms on controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
